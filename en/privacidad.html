Privacy of your catches

What we do with what you record in your fishing log and with payment data. Last updated: 22 October 2026.

Your log is private

The trips, catches, photos, notes and locations you save in your log are not shown to other users. The only exception is the private groups you join, which see what you have enabled to share with them for each type of trip.

Anonymous statistics and fishing index

We use the catches in your log, anonymously and in aggregate, to improve the fishing index and our statistics. Your catches at catalogue spots add to counters (species, spot and month) that do not record who caught what, and the index's learning only publishes summaries from at least 5 different people. Catches at your personal locations never add to the counters.

Legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): improving the service with data that does not identify you. You can object at any time in 👤 → My subscription → “Your data” (untick the box), and from that moment we stop using your catches for this. Whatever had already been added to a counter cannot be separated, because the counter does not record who it was. We explain this in the terms sheet you accept when you create your account.

Share your catch

This is different from the anonymous statistics above: it is voluntary and chosen catch by catch. Each catch has a box “Share this catch anonymously”, unticked by default. Only if you tick it do we save an anonymous copy for the community and to improve the fishing index.

Species, length (rounded to the cm), weight (rounded to 50 g), technique, type of trip (shore, boat, spearfishing), date and time (without minutes), approximate area (a cell of about 5 km, never the exact point; if you fished at a catalogue spot, that public spot) and the trip conditions (tide, pressure, wind, swell, water temperature, moon). Your name, your email or any data from your account; the exact point or the name of your personal locations; handwritten notes, bait or lure; photos.

The anonymous copy does not carry your user account. Nobody reads them one by one: only summaries are shown (for example, “sea bass in this area in October in the morning”), and a summary only appears if it is made up of catches from at least 5 different people. That way nobody can deduce who caught something at a place with few people.

So that you can withdraw it whenever you like, we keep an internal link between your catch and its anonymous copy. That link can only be seen by you and is not used for anything else.

How to withdraw it

  • Edit the catch and untick the box: the anonymous copy is deleted at that moment.
  • If you delete the catch, the trip or your account, its anonymous copy is deleted too.
  • Whatever had already been counted in a summary stops counting as soon as the copy is deleted.

Open data from other people

To check the fishing index we also use public observations of fish and cephalopods published under an open licence on GBIF (including those from iNaturalist). Only those their author published as CC0 or CC BY; CC BY-NC ones are kept separately as a reference and are not used in the app. We respect the obscured location each observer chose (we never try to refine it) and we only keep the author's name when the licence requires attribution.

How you found us and visits to the website

To know what works to make ourselves known, we count visits to the public pages (tides, spots, species, the landing page and the sign-in page) only as a number per day, type of page and where they come from (for example “Instagram”, “Google” or “direct”). We do not store your IP, your browser, the page you come from or any identifier, and we do not use cookies or third-party trackers for this.

When you create an account, we store with it how you first arrived: the source (from a closed list: Instagram, Facebook, Google, a friend…), the medium and the campaign name (for example “20261013-condiciones-bakio”). Until you create the account, that data stays only in your browser (for up to 90 days). We use it solely to count, in total, how many sign-ups each channel brings.

Invite a friend

If you share your “Invite a friend” link, we store that the account was created with your link so that we can give you the free month when they pay. You only see how many friends have signed up and how many months you have earned, never who they are. So that each friend counts only once we store an encrypted summary (hash) of their email, not the email; and to prevent abuse we check with our payment provider (Stripe) whether payment is made with the same card, storing only another encrypted summary of the card's fingerprint, never its number.

Payments and subscription

If you subscribe to Premium, the charge is made by our payment provider, Stripe, which receives your email, your name, your billing address and your card details. We never see or store the card number. With your account we store only what is necessary to provide the service and comply with the tax authorities: the Stripe customer and subscription identifier, the plan (monthly or annual, regional or Worldwide, founder), its status and dates, the currency, the billing country (it determines VAT), the pricing zone and the promotional code you have used. To charge you the price for your area we also store the country that issued your card (provided by Stripe; never the number) and, while you pay by card, the country (only the 2-letter code, never the IP address or location) from which you open the app each day, for the last 14 days only; if your card or your ongoing use belong to another area, we email you and from the next renewal you pay that area's price. Invoices are kept by Stripe, and we keep the billing data for the periods required by tax law.

The emails about your free trial (welcome, a notice 3 days before and end of the trial) and about your subscription (a notice before the annual renewal) are service notices about your own account, not advertising. The subscription conditions, automatic renewal, cancellation and withdrawal are set out in the terms and conditions.

Backups

So that your log is not lost if something fails, we make a backup of the database every night. It is stored encrypted on GitHub, only we can open it and it is deleted after 30 days: if you delete something or delete your account, it also disappears from the backups within that period at most.

Controller, processors and your rights

Controller: Costaviva (costaviva.org). Owner: to be completed (name, tax ID (NIF) and address). Contact: datos@costaviva.org.

Legal basis: the contract with you (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for the account and the service; legal obligations (art. 6(1)(c)) for billing; and our legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)) for improving the app and for the anonymous statistics. We keep your data for as long as you have the account; billing data, for the years required by tax law.

Processors (they process data only to provide us with the service): Supabase (database and access), Cloudflare (website hosting and CAPTCHA), Stripe (payments), Resend (emails), Anthropic (identifying the species in the photo you send) and GitHub (encrypted backup). Some are outside the European Union and process the data with the safeguards required by the GDPR. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

To exercise your rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability) over any data in your account, write to datos@costaviva.org. If you believe we do not respect them, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) (aepd.es).
